**Weight 50%. Due Friday of week 12, 5:00pm.** Demonstration assessment — the
date and the mark on this site are fictional.

One program of your own choosing, roughly 150–300 lines. It must:

- read some data, do something with it, and report a result;
- be split into named functions, each of which you can describe in a sentence;
- come with a test plan: what you checked, with what inputs, and what you
  expected;
- include an account of **one bug**, from the symptom to the cause to the fix.

The bug account is not a confession, it is evidence. A project with no bug
account reads as a project that was never really tested.

## Marking

Holistic, out of 50. Ambition is not rewarded on its own — a small program that
is correct, tested and explained scores above a large one that mostly works.
