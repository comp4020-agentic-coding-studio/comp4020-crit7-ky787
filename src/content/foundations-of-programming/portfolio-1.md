**Weight 20%. Due Friday of week 5, 5:00pm.** Demonstration assessment — the
date and the mark on this site are fictional.

Submit four short programs from weeks 1 to 5, each with a paragraph that says:

1. what you expected the program to do;
2. what it actually did the first time you ran it;
3. what you changed, and why that fixed it.

Point 2 is the one that carries the marks. A portfolio entry where the program
worked first time and nothing is said about it is worth less than one where
something broke and you can explain it.

## Marking

Holistic, out of 20. Working code with no account of how it got there is a pass,
not a distinction.
