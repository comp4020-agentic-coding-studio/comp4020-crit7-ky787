**Weight 10%. Due Friday of week 6, 5:00pm.** Demonstration assessment — the
date and the mark on this site are fictional.

A short piece, deliberately early, so that the habit is in place before A2.

## What to submit

Two pages. Three claims, each handled in the same four moves:

1. State the claim as published.
2. Name the representation, the specimen and the optimisation level it is about.
3. Say what it does **not** establish, in one sentence beginning "this does not
   show that".
4. Name the single piece of evidence that would settle the remaining doubt, and
   say whether this corpus contains it.

## The three claims

- *"The substitution pass expanded every site in the target."*
- *"The graph got smaller, so the deobfuscation worked."*
- *"The lifted module passed the LLVM verifier, so the lift is faithful."*

Two of the three are refuted somewhere in this course's own corpus. Finding
where is part of the exercise.

## Marking

Holistic, out of 10. A script that answers all four moves for all three claims
and is wrong about one of them scores better than one that answers move 1 and
move 4 only.
